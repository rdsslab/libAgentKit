Jesus: The Story of a Kingdom

Jesus, born in the early 1st century CE in the Kingdom of Judah, was a central figure in the history of the Christian tradition. His life, teachings, and death are central to Christian faith, symbolizing humanity's journey toward salvation.

Biography/Timeline

Jesus was born in the region of Galilee, in the city of Nazareth. He lived in the time of Christ, dedicated to the Kingdom of God, and taught about love, humility, and the sacrifice of the cross.

Teachings

Jesus emphasized love, the importance of humility, and the idea of a Kingdom of God. His teachings influenced millions and became foundational to Christianity.

Legacy/Significance

Jesus' life and death are central to Christian faith, representing humanity's journey toward salvation. His crucifixion and resurrection are seen as pivotal events in the history of the Christian tradition.
